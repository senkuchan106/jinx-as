STAFF APPLICATIONMajor role • 15++
  • Must have Major role.
  • Must be 15 years old or above.
  • Moderation experience is not required, but it's a plus.
  • Must be sound minded and mature.
  • Must be active.
  • Must have more than 12+ hours VC time.
  • Be active at least 4 hours a day.
  • Joined the server for 1 month or above.
  • Be professional with tickets, VCs and clan mates.
  • Must follow requirements, rules and higher staff instructions.
RECRUITER APPLICATIONSergeant+ • 14++
  • Must have Sergeant role or above.
  • Must be 14 years old or above.
  • Must be active and friendly.
  • Must have recruiting experience.
  • Must be professional and not toxic.
  • Must have more than 8+ hours VC time.
  • Must be active at least 4–5 hours daily.
  • Must follow clan rules.
  • Must complete 25 recruits per month quota.
  • At least 10 recruited players need to be active.
  • Must respect higher staff.
